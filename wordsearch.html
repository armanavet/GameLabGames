    How to play

    The objective of this game is to find all the listed words hidden in the grid. To mark words you have found, tap or click the first letter of the word, drag until the last letter and release. Words can run horizontally, vertically or diagonally and may go forward or backward. A useful strategy to find a word is to look for its first letter and then look in all eight possible directions.

    Save

    Your progress will automatically be saved every 10 seconds. Additionally, you can tap Save from the navigation menu at any time.

    Hint

    Use the Hint option under Assist at the top of the puzzle to show the first letter of a word that is not yet found. To find a specific word, click or tap on the word first and then click on Hint.

    Reveal

    If you are stuck, use the Reveal option under Assist at the top of the puzzle to see all the answers.

    Start again

    Use the Start again option to reset the entire grid. The empty grid will be displayed and the clock will be set back to zero.

    Print

    Use the Print menu to print the empty puzzle, the grid with found words, or the solution grid.

    Timer

    The timer can be turned on or off. Click on Settings to find the switch.

    Sound

    You can turn off game sounds via the Settings menu if you’d rather not have sound while solving the puzzle.
