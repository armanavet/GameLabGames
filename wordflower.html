    How to play

    Make as many words as you can from the seven letters of the flower. Every word must use the letter in the center and be at least four letters long. Letters can be used more than once in the same word.

    Tap the petals or type on your keyboard to spell a word, then press Enter. Words you find are listed beside the flower; tap one to look up its meaning.

    Score

    A four-letter word is worth 1 point, and every letter after that adds a point: five letters score 2, six score 3, and so on. A pangram, a word that uses all seven letters, earns 5 bonus points. Your score moves you along the stages at the top, from Newbie to Golden Poppy.

    Hint

    The bulb shows the first letter of a word you have not found yet, and how long it is. Tap the eye to uncover more of its letters; the last one is always yours to find. Tap the bulb again for a different word. Hints never cost you points.

    Shuffle

    Moves the petals around to help you see new words. The center letter stays put.

    Reveal

    Shows every word you have not found and ends the puzzle. Your score stays what it was before you revealed.

    Save

    Your progress is saved automatically in this browser. You can also choose Save from the menu at any time.

    Start again

    Clears your words and sets the clock back to zero.
