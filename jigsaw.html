How to play

Press Start game to break the picture into pieces, then put it back together. Drag a piece next to one it belongs beside and the two click together; from then on they move as one. Keep joining until the whole picture is one piece.

Image

Shows the finished picture in the corner of the table. Tap the picture, or the button again, to put it away.

Edges only

Hides the pieces from the middle of the picture so you can build the border first. Press All pieces to bring them back.

Hint

Wiggles two pieces that fit together.

Save

Your progress is saved automatically in this browser, and you can choose Save from the menu at any time.

Start again

Puts the picture back together and sets the clock back to zero, ready to start over.
